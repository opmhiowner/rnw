Listing description

1. Waialae Place is a charming 2 bedroom 1 bath apartment located at the edge of Kaimuki and Moiliili. This partly furnished unit consists of approximately 607 square feet plus lanai and one parking stall.

2. The condominium address is 2845 Waialae Avenue. Waialae Place is located conveniently near Market City Shopping Center, The University of Hawaii and nearby an array of dining and entertainment options.

3. This unit has a kitchen complete with refrigerator, range, and disposal. Air conditioning for your comfort is also located in the living room. Amenities for this building include a swimming pool, BBQ area, elevator, coin laundry and visitor parking.

4. Rent for this unit includes water and sewer. Electricity and TV cable are tenant's expense.

5. Drive north on Kapiolani Boulevard till it meets Waialae Avenue. At the traffic light, turn left and stay in your far left lane. Waialae Place will be on your left. Go up the ramp and park in visitor parking. Wait at the entrance of the pool area for our rental agent.

6. Once again, the address is 2845 Waialae Avenue.
